How to paste it

Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new (or select your existing one).
  4. Click inside the editor box and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies.
  6. Click Save Changes at the bottom of the page.

Outlook (desktop)

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Click New, name it, click in the editing box and paste.
  4. Set it as default for new messages and replies, then click OK.

Apple Mail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Mail → Settings → Signatures and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, then paste into the right-hand box.
  4. Close the window and choose the signature when writing a message.

If the images don’t appear after pasting, check your internet connection and paste again. Send a test email to yourself to check the result.
